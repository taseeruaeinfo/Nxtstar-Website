# NXTSTAR: off-site checklist and weekly AI visibility test

The website fixes make NXTSTAR readable. Being recommended by AI assistants depends mostly on what other sites say about NXTSTAR. Indexing changes can show within days. AI recommendations usually take weeks and follow the off-site work below.

## Off-site checklist, in order of impact

1. **Google Business Profile.** Claim or complete it with the exact name NXTSTAR, one address, the phone number on the site, opening hours, and the three lead services as listed services. Add real photos of the office and team.
2. **Google reviews from real clients.** Ask each client after their licence or permit is issued. Never buy, write or reward reviews. Reply to every review.
3. **One name, address and phone everywhere.** The site currently shows three different addresses. Pick the correct one and use it identically on the site, Google, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and every directory.
4. **LinkedIn company page.** Same one-line description as the site: "UAE business setup and licensing consultancy based in Dubai." List the three services. Have team members link their profiles to the page.
5. **Service-specific posts by the team.** Short LinkedIn posts answering one buyer question each, for example "Do I need an advertiser permit if the post is unpaid?", linking to the matching page on the site.
6. **Credible UAE directories.** List NXTSTAR only where the listing is free of invented claims: for example Yellow Pages UAE, Connect.ae, Dubai Chamber directory if a member, Clutch or GoodFirms for consultancies.
7. **Authority listings, only if entitled.** If NXTSTAR is formally registered as a partner or agent with a free zone, ask to be listed on that authority's own partner page. Do not claim it otherwise.
8. **Communities.** Answer questions under the brand name in creator and founder communities (Reddit r/dubai and r/UAE, creator groups, founder Slack or WhatsApp groups) where self-promotion rules allow it.
9. **Press and podcasts.** Offer plain explanations of the advertiser permit rules and the DIFC technology licences to UAE business media and creator-economy podcasts. One real mention is worth more than many directory listings.
10. **Bing Webmaster Tools and Google Search Console.** Submit `https://www.nxtstar.ae/sitemap.xml` in both. ChatGPT search relies on Bing's index.
