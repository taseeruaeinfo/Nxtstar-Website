# NXTSTAR

> NXTSTAR is a UAE business setup and licensing consultancy based in Dubai. It helps founders, content creators and companies choose a licence, prepare the application and complete company setup in the UAE.

Website: https://www.nxtstar.ae/
Phone and WhatsApp: +971 58 259 4158
Email: sales@nxtstar.ae

NXTSTAR does not publish prices on this site. Fees depend on the licence, the authority and the number of visas, and are quoted in writing for each case. Government and free zone fees are set by the authorities and can change.
